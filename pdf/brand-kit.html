The split is the identity. Mono carries every number, path, hostname, key cap, version and eyebrow label. Sans carries prose. That one rule is what makes these documents read as instrumentation rather than as marketing, and it is the first thing lost in a restyle.

Product mark

qsarflex-logo.svg, vector. This is the same file the application serves from the public assets CDN, so the documents cannot drift from the product. Use the SVG, never a raster: it is set at 21 mm on a cover and as a 172 mm watermark, where a bitmap shows its edges.

House rules
PageA4, 210 × 297 mm, 17 mm side margins. Section head with a 0.5 pt rule under it; running foot on every page but the cover.
No em dashes in proseA comma, colon, semicolon, full stop or brackets instead. The only — left is the standalone glyph meaning "not applicable" in a table cell.
Do not explain the obviousState the constraint and stop. An IT reader does not need the arithmetic worked through for them.
Never mention betaThe pre-release channel is closed and is not offered publicly.
Watch the page footThe HTML build fails a page whose content runs past A4. Canva gives no such warning, so check the foot of any page you edit.
Setting this up in Canva

Brand → Brand Kit → new kit named QSAR Flex. Add the colours above as a palette, then upload the eight Geist TTFs from fonts/ under Brand fonts and set Geist 600 as the heading font and Geist 400 as the body font. Upload both logos. Brand kits need Canva Pro.

Do the font upload before importing the PDFs. If Canva substitutes its own fonts on import, every line re-wraps and the layout has to be rebuilt by hand.
