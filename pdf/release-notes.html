A new interface
Section1

In 4.0 the desktop application's interface is replaced with the one QSAR Flex uses on the web, wrapped in a native application for Windows and macOS. Nothing you have already done is affected: your account, your licence and your validated results carry over as they are.

N‑Nitroso‑sertraline, with its nitrosamine centre marked. ChemiGraphy draws every structure in 4.0, on screen and in every report, in the theme you are using.

Everything is in one bar. A single bar across the top carries Load and Curate on the left, a Search box in the middle, and Evaluate, your licence status, documentation and the theme on the right. An empty workspace offers its loading choices on a card instead.

Load, curate and evaluate on one table. Every compound is checked as it arrives and wears its verdict on the row. A panel on the left holds everything about one row, and ChemiGraphy opens from it to draw or correct a structure. Nothing moves between screens.

Search for the item you want. Press ⌘K or Ctrl K and type. The command bar searches every action in the product and runs it from wherever you are, naming where each control lives. It is the fastest way to find a feature you cannot see.

The workspace. Left to right along the bar: Load and Curate, the Search box that the command bar opens behind, the count of what the workspace holds, Evaluate, your licence status, the documentation button and the light or dark toggle. Under the heading, undo, redo and a Clear button; below, one row per compound with its verdict, name, CAS and SMILES. Click a row and its panel opens on the left.

What is new
Section2
QSAR Flex now also runs on macOS
A native Apple Silicon build, signed by Apple and notarised, installs by dragging it to Applications and needs no administrator rights. It is the same application as the Windows one: the same interface, the same modules, the same reports, and both now keep your sign-in between launches.
Platform
MultiCASE's structure editor, built into QSAR Flex
Draw a compound instead of typing its SMILES, or open a compound the check flagged and correct it on the canvas: Load, then Draw, or Edit structure on any row. The whole editor is there, with its element and bond palettes, periodic table, ring library, lookup by name, wedges and arrows. What you draw is read by the same engine that checks and evaluates your compounds, so the structure that reaches the table is exactly the one QSAR Flex scores. ChemiGraphy also draws every structure you see, on screen and in every report.
ChemiGraphy
One table: drop a file anywhere, or paste structures
Drop files onto the page or paste SMILES directly; the file extension picks the importer, so compounds and reactions in one drop are separated for you. Every compound is checked as it arrives and wears its verdict on the row. Click a row and a panel opens beside the table with the structure, the finding, the results and the history; a Results column links each module to its report.
Workspace
Real undo, and bulk correction you can inspect
Curation keeps a fifty-step history and every step is named, so the menu tells you what will come back before you press it. One Step Cure is a single dialog with four counted decisions, plus optional passes for chiral tags, charge neutralisation and PubChem verification. A mixture is split into its components or reduced to the part with the most atoms; duplicates are removed outright or collapsed to one per group. Any row can be swapped for one of its tautomers: the panel generates up to 200, shows them beside the parent, and adopts the one you pick. A bulk run finishes in a summary you can filter down to the rows that need attention.
Curation
Long runs report what they are doing, and can be cancelled
Evaluation, curation and file loading run behind a progress overlay that names the phase it is in rather than showing a percentage. All three can be cancelled, and cancelling says what was left alone. If a run returns fewer results than it was given, a banner says so and names the counts, so a short run is never quietly presented as a complete one.
Progress
An Account page, and licence activity you can read back
Account gathers Profile, Security, Licence and Team in one place. The chip in the navigation bar shows tests remaining, Unlimited, or that a licence has expired, and opens the same page in a click. Each licence has an activity page listing every evaluation with its user, modules, item count and billing state, which is what an invoice reconciles against. Enterprise administrators assign and remove seats and create users themselves, without involving MultiCASE.
Licensing
A support portal, which did not exist before
Until now support meant an email thread. support.multicase.com is new in this release. It is a site of its own: open it in a browser and sign in with the MultiCASE account you use for QSAR Flex, with no separate registration. Raise a ticket for anything, access, licensing, seats, bugs or questions, attach whatever shows the problem, and follow it to resolution with its status visible. Replies come back in the thread. You can also email support@multicase.com, but the portal is the preferred route.

What has not changed
Section3

The interface is new. The chemistry underneath it is not. If you have validated a QSAR Flex result, 4.0 does not change it.

The models
The same statistical models, expert alerts, read-across and rule-based methods, with the same applicability-domain and uncertainty handling.
The endpoints
The same catalogue: sixteen modules in four bundles, Nitrosamine, Ecotoxicity, Physicochemical and ADME.
The results
Predictions, reports and their supporting evidence come from the same engine. Every record count and every number a model returns is the same.
The licence types
Individual or Enterprise coverage, and still Subscription, On-Demand Subscription and Pay-Per-Test, with the same module and bundle entitlements.
Sign-in and data
Still your MultiCASE account. There is no data migration and no licence re-issue, and the desktop application still evaluates on your own machine.
You also have a web application

The interface in 4.0 is the one QSAR Flex has been running in the browser, and your licence covers it. Sign in at qsarflex.multicase.com with the same account: nothing to install, nothing to download, and it stays current on its own. It suits people who move between machines, or a colleague who needs occasional access without a deployment.

Two differences are worth knowing. The web application sends your structures to MultiCASE to be evaluated, where the desktop evaluates them on your own machine and sends none. And Surrogate Search and Cross Similarity are desktop-only, because they read across the whole reference database rather than fetching one record. If structures may not leave your network, stay on the desktop.

Propranolol. On the desktop a structure like this one is evaluated on your machine and goes nowhere. On the web it travels to MultiCASE for the length of the evaluation and is not kept.
Getting started on 4.0

The installation guides, a walkthrough of the new interface and the full model catalogue are on resources.multicase.com. Anything else, open a ticket with the account you already use.
